A Wood Art Synergy

Hello, and welcome to Creative & Celtic! Our journey into professional wood artistry began in 2020 following my husband's back injury, which required a career change. His lifelong passion for woodworking opened a new door for us, leading us to establish our home-based business right here in the Bitterroot Valley.

What started as a shared enjoyment of crafting spiritual items has grown organically, primarily through word-of-mouth. From small decorative bowls and trays to custom police and military plaques and personalized wall decorations, we discovered how much joy our creations bring to others. We are deeply inspired by the insight of our community and clients, and we thrive on bringing unique visions to life. We look forward to hearing from you and discussing the perfect custom project for any occasion— or just because.
