Hello, and Welcome to Creative & Celtic!

We began tinkering in wood art in 2020 when my husband got hurt driving truck. His back injury prevented him from working traditional jobs, and his love of woodworking opened up a new door for him; we decided it was time to try working from home, hence- Creative & Celtic!

We began by making spiritual things we liked, and that others came to see and like, shipping internationally, at first, then began making little bowls, then bigger bowls, and posted them online. Our friends started wanting them, too, along with trays. Then our friends started asking us to make projects for them, like police and military plaques, wall decorations, tarot card boxes, you name it. Then, Marty went to Summit in Stevensville, and several more people began wanting our creations; just by word of mouth, we were very busy! Then, we moved and things slowed down for awhile until we could make our transition work, and here we are! Ready to talk to you on a project you would like, whether for an occasion, or just because. All the best.
